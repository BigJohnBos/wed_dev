LAGOON

Lagoon is a very fun place to go and if you are in utah you should visit. It is an amazing amusement park for all ages. From roller coasters, to kitty rides and ferris wheels, lagoon has fun for all. If you visit, a couple of rides you should ride are:

  • Wicked
  • Cannabal
  • The Skyride
  • Colossus

Reasons to go

  • Big park
  • Fun rides
  • Large variety of rides
  • Free rides once you are in

One of the best rides there is Cannibal. It is a very fast roller coaster with a really big drop. Even though most times it has a long wait, it is worth it.
